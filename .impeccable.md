## Design Context

### Users
Working professionals (IT auditors, IS risk managers, compliance officers, internal auditors) primarily in their 30s–40s. They study in evenings and weekends after demanding workdays. Most are employed at Big 4 firms, financial institutions, government agencies, or enterprise tech companies. High-stakes certification — career advancement depends on passing. They want to feel the platform is credible and worth their limited time.

### Brand Personality
**Precise. Authoritative. Structured.** Like a Bloomberg terminal designed by people who actually passed the exam. Not startup-y or playful. Not dull academic software either — confident, organized, and clear about what it delivers.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Light mode** for the marketing/landing page (studied during coffee breaks, lunch, morning prep sessions). Dark sections used intentionally and sparingly for maximum contrast — stats section, pricing, test preview only.

The aesthetic should read as premium professional services, not SaaS startup. Think BCG consulting tools, Bloomberg, Deloitte Learning — clean, information-respecting, quietly authoritative.

Anti-references: Duolingo (too playful), generic SaaS with purple gradient CTAs, typical "EdTech" with pastel illustrations.

### Typography
- **Display/Headings**: Bricolage Grotesque (`--font-bricolage`) — weights 400, 500, 600
- **Body/UI**: Figtree (`--font-figtree`) — weights 400, 500, 600
- Applied via CSS variables: `--font-heading: var(--font-bricolage)`, `--font-sans: var(--font-figtree)`
- Fluid heading sizes: clamp where appropriate, `text-balance` on H1
- Body text capped at `max-w-[52ch]` to `max-w-[72ch]` depending on context

### Color System (OKLCH)
- **Primary (brand purple)**: `oklch(0.62 0.18 275)` — accents, CTAs, primary actions
- **Background (tinted near-white)**: `oklch(0.99 0.006 275)` — subtle purple tint for cohesion
- **Foreground**: `oklch(0.13 0.015 275)`
- **Muted foreground**: `oklch(0.52 0.012 275)`
- **Border**: `oklch(0.91 0.01 275)`
- **Light lavender accent** (on dark backgrounds): `oklch(0.78 0.14 275)` — number counters
- **Stats/dark section text**: same lavender `oklch(0.78 0.14 275)` on `bg-zinc-950`

### Design Principles
1. **Authority through restraint** — don't over-design; trust the content and data
2. **Specificity over genericism** — use real company names (Deloitte, KPMG, EY, JPMorgan), actual exam domain names, specific percentages (94%, not 90%)
3. **Asymmetry as hierarchy** — not everything deserves equal weight; feature the most important stat larger
4. **Motion that informs** — animations reveal structure and confirm state changes; perpetual micro-animations on the hero mockup make it feel alive
5. **Zero gradient text** — no rainbow fills; typography communicates through weight, scale, and solid color only

### Absolute Bans (enforced)
- NO gradient text (`background-clip: text` + gradient) — anywhere
- NO side-stripe borders (`border-left > 1px`) on cards/alerts
- NO 3-column equal-weight card grids for feature sections
- NO centered hero layout (split screen enforced)
- NO generic tech company logos in social proof (use company names of actual CSCA audience orgs)

### Layout Patterns Established
- **Hero**: `grid lg:grid-cols-[55fr_45fr]` split screen — content left, dashboard mockup right with float animation
- **Stats**: `grid lg:grid-cols-[3fr_2fr]` — featured large stat left, two smaller stacked right
- **Testimonials**: featured full-width + `grid-cols-[1fr_1fr]` asymmetric secondary (2 stacked left, 1 taller right)
- **How It Works**: zig-zag `flex md:flex-row` with `md:flex-row-reverse` on even steps; each step has contextual mini-UI instead of abstract icon
- **Social proof**: scrolling text marquee of real company names, no logo images

### Motion System
- **Easing**: `EASE_OUT_EXPO = [0.16, 1, 0.3, 1]` for all scroll reveals
- **Spring**: `stiffness: 260, damping: 38` for VerticalCutReveal; `stiffness: 400, damping: 20` for hover
- **Float loop**: `animate={{ y: [0, -7, 0] }}` with `duration: 5, ease: "easeInOut", repeat: Infinity` — hero mockup
- **Stat card pulse**: `animate={{ opacity: [1, 0.75, 1] }}` staggered per card
- **Progress bars**: `initial={{ width: 0 }} animate={{ width: "X%" }}` with EASE_OUT_EXPO on mount
- `VerticalCutReveal` on ALL section headings (spring stiffness 260, damping 38)
- `active:scale-[0.97]` on all CTA buttons for tactile feedback
